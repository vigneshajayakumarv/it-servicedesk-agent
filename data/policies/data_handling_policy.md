# Data Handling and Incident Policy

Sensitive data includes salary, payroll, personal, and HR information. Such data must only be shared with authorised recipients through approved channels.

If sensitive data is sent to the wrong recipient or exposed by mistake, it is a data incident. The employee must report it to the security team immediately. The service desk escalates all suspected data exposure to security without attempting to resolve it directly, and where possible initiates a message recall.

Suspected phishing emails should be reported and not actioned. Employees must not click links or enter credentials, and should forward the suspicious email to the security team for analysis.
