# Onboarding and Offboarding Policy

New hire setup is initiated by the hiring manager and must be completed before the start date, including account creation, email, device allocation, and required group access.

When an employee leaves, their manager initiates offboarding. The service desk disables the leaver's email and system access within 24 hours of their departure and revokes building and application access. Offboarding actions are treated as security-sensitive and require confirmation before execution.
