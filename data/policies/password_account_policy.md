# Password and Account Access Policy

Employees who are locked out after multiple failed sign-in attempts can have their account unlocked by the service desk once identity is verified. Verification requires confirming the employee's ID and one additional detail on file, such as manager name or assigned device.

Password resets are self-service through the company portal where possible. The service desk may issue a temporary password only after identity verification, and the user must change it on first sign-in.

MFA resets are required when an employee loses the device holding their authenticator app. The service desk resets MFA enrolment after verifying identity, and the user re-enrols on a replacement device. Privileged or administrator accounts require security team approval before any password or MFA reset.
